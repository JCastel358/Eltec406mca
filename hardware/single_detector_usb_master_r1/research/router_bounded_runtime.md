`router.optimizer.max_passes` exists as a serialized field, but `BatchOptimizer.runBatchLoop()` does not use it. `-mp` limits routing passes. The optimizer continues according to its separate improvement threshold or a stop request. Therefore do not treat `--router.optimizer.max_passes=1` as an enforced pass cap in this revision. [Optimizer fields](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/20f1a72e546b9b23c7ba5127086885cfacbdd4be/src/main/java/app/freerouting/settings/RouterOptimizerSettings.java#L14), [actual loop](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/20f1a72e546b9b23c7ba5127086885cfacbdd4be/src/main/java/app/freerouting/autoroute/BatchOptimizer.java#L58).

Repeated displayed scores do not establish identical stopping metrics. The optimizer measures a combined relative improvement in via count and trace length; its log prints a separately calculated normalized score. On the first pass returning zero improvement while increased rip-up costs remain enabled, it disables those costs and returns `-1`, deliberately forcing another pass. Initial `route_improved=-1` also forces the first pass. Thus identical displayed scores on passes 1 and 2 can be consistent with continuing work; the log alone does not reveal the exact threshold result. [Improvement calculation and forced retry](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/20f1a72e546b9b23c7ba5127086885cfacbdd4be/src/main/java/app/freerouting/autoroute/BatchOptimizer.java#L117).

A future overall soft deadline can be set with `--router.job_timeout=00:08:00`, beneath the wrapper's 600-second hard limit. The scheduler parses the timespan, polls the deadline and requests a graceful stop, with a 30-second grace period. This is a whole-job deadline, not a strict optimizer-pass duration. Keep the external hard timeout longer and retain the native SES/content/import checks.
